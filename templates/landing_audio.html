How to Convert YouTube to MP3

  1. Copy the URL of any YouTube video.
  2. Paste it into the input field above and click Get Video.
  3. Select Audio Only (MP3) from the format dropdown.
  4. Click Download and wait for the conversion to complete.
  5. Save the MP3 file to your device.

Audio Quality

Audio is extracted and converted to MP3 at 192kbps quality. This provides a good balance between file size and audio fidelity. FFmpeg is required for the conversion process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What audio format is used?

Audio is converted to MP3 at 192kbps, which is compatible with virtually all devices and media players.

Do I need FFmpeg installed?

Yes. FFmpeg is required for the audio extraction and conversion process. Without it, only video downloads will work.

Is there a limit on video length?

There is no explicit length limit, but very long videos may take more time to process and convert.
